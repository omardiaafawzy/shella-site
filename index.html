Your shella, far from home.

Shella is a student app for the New Alamein campuses, starting with the AASTMT Alamein branch. It helps students get home together and find each other on campus.

Ride home

Students going to Sidi Gaber, Alexandria, fill one 14-seat minibus together. The fare is 150 EGP a seat, paid to the driver in cash. Nothing is paid in the app.

Hangouts

Join or start activities with other students: sports, the beach, study groups, food and trips.

Campus map

See friends and hangouts on a map of New Alamein. Students choose who sees them, or hide completely.

For drivers

When a minibus is full, Shella sends the assigned driver one WhatsApp message with the pickup time and place. Only drivers who agreed to it get these messages, and they never include students' details.
